PROJECT: Customer Sales & Business Performance Analytics

OBJECTIVE:

To analyze customer sales and business performance data using
Python, SQL and Power BI to identify revenue trends,
customer behavior, product performance, regional performance
and profitability opportunities.

BUSINESS QUESTIONS:

1. What is the total revenue?
2. What is the total profit?
3. Which products perform best?
4. Which customers generate the highest revenue?
5. Which regions perform best?
6. How are monthly sales changing?
7. How do discounts affect profitability?
8. Which products have low profit margins?
9. What percentage of customers are returning customers?
10. What business recommendations can be made from the analysis?
